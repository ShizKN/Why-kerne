Why Kerne

I'm a 16-year-old student from Morocco, and honestly, one of my biggest problems with studying is just getting started.

Studying alone gets boring pretty quickly, and it's way too easy to get distracted. But I've noticed that when other people are working around me, I tend to focus a lot better.

So I thought, why not bring that online?

That's how Kerne started. I wanted a simple place where students could join study rooms, work alongside their friends, and actually get things done together.

I built the first version over a weekend using AI coding tools. I'm not an expert programmer, but AI made it possible for me to turn an idea into something real without spending months learning everything from scratch.

It's still far from perfect, but I think that's what I enjoy most about building with AI: you can have a random idea on Friday and actually have something working by Sunday.

Hopefully, Kerne makes studying a little less painful for someone else too.
